# Ziggy's ABC Adventure! — Voiceover Script

## Opening

Ziggy:
"Hello, my little friends! 👋 Are you ready to learn the ABCs with me? Let’s go!"

## Song Intro

Ziggy:
"A is for Apple, red and round,
B is for Ball, bouncing on the ground!
C is for Cat, meow, meow, meow,
Come learn your ABCs with Ziggy now!"

## A — Apple

Ziggy:
"A! A is for Apple!"
Pause.
"Apple, apple, apple!"
"Can you say it with me?"

## B — Ball

Ziggy:
"B! B is for Ball!"
Pause.
"Ball, ball, ball!"
"Can you find the ball?"

## C — Cat

Ziggy:
"C! C is for Cat!"
Pause.
"Cat, cat, cat!"
"Meow!"
"Can you say CAT?"

## D — Dog

Ziggy:
"D! D is for Dog!"
Pause.
"Dog, dog, dog!"
"Can you say DOG?"

## E — Elephant

Ziggy:
"E! E is for Elephant!"
Pause.
"Elephant, elephant, elephant!"

## F — Fish

Ziggy:
"F! F is for Fish!"
Pause.
"Fish, fish, fish!"

## G — Grapes

Ziggy:
"G! G is for Grapes!"
Pause.
"Grapes, grapes, grapes!"

## H — Hat

Ziggy:
"H! H is for Hat!"
Pause.
"Hat, hat, hat!"

## I — Ice cream

Ziggy:
"I! I is for Ice cream!"
Pause.
"Ice cream, ice cream, ice cream!"

## J — Juice

Ziggy:
"J! J is for Juice!"
Pause.
"Juice, juice, juice!"

## Review

Ziggy:
"Wow! Look what we learned! A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J!"

Ziggy:
"A-B-C, come sing with me!
Learning is fun as fun can be!
A-B-C, one more time,
Sing your ABCs and shine!"

## Closing

Ziggy:
"You did a great job, little learners! Keep learning, keep smiling, and I’ll see you next time! Bye-bye! 👋🐒"

## Ending Card

"GREAT JOB! ⭐ SEE YOU NEXT TIME WITH ZIGGY! 🐒"
