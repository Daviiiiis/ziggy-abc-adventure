# Ziggy's ABC Adventure! — Storyboard

## Project Overview

Title: "Ziggy’s ABC Adventure! 🎵🍎"
Format: 3D animated preschool educational short
Audience: Ages 2–6
Style: Bright, colorful, safe, playful, polished children's TV quality
Mood: Warm, cheerful, energetic, friendly, encouraging

---

## Scene 1 — Opening Park Arrival

### Shot 1: Wide establishing shot
- Camera: Slow dolly-in from a wide aerial view of the magical children's park
- Visuals: Green grass, colorful flowers, friendly trees, butterflies, small clouds, floating bubbles, bright blue sky
- Audio: Gentle upbeat melody begins with soft percussion and xylophone

### Shot 2: Ziggy enters frame
- Camera: Medium shot, low angle, slight forward motion
- Visuals: Ziggy happily jumps into the park, waving at the camera with a big smile
- Ziggy action: energetic little hop, arms raised, cheerful bounce
- Audio: Soft claps and bell notes

### Shot 3: Close-up on Ziggy face
- Camera: Close-up, gentle zoom-in
- Ziggy dialogue: "Hello, my little friends! 👋 Are you ready to learn the ABCs with me? Let’s go!"
- Expression: bright eyes, excited grin

### Shot 4: Ziggy claps
- Camera: Medium shot
- Ziggy action: claps hands twice, then stands poised to begin
- Audio: Crisp clap sound effect, playful rhythm

## Timing Notes

- Total runtime target: approximately 2–3 minutes
- Keep each letter scene short and clear
- Give children time to repeat words aloud
- Use gentle pauses after key phrases
- Avoid clutter; keep each object and letter visually distinct
- Maintain smooth transitions and consistent character design

---

## Visual Continuity Checklist

- Ziggy’s fur, face, clothing, and shoes remain exactly consistent throughout
- Bright yellow shirt, blue shorts, red sneakers remain unchanged
- Warm, soft, cheerful park environment remains cohesive
- Large uppercase letters are clear and easy to read
- All objects are simple, familiar, and child-friendly
- Facial expressions remain friendly and joyful

---

## Educational Emphasis

- Letters and words are pronounced slowly and clearly
- Important words repeat multiple times
- Learning interactions invite participation
- Each object is easy to recognize and memorable
- No scary elements, no loud sudden sounds, no confusing transitions

---

## Production Direction Summary

This storyboard is designed to feel like a polished educational preschool cartoon with an upbeat, welcoming tone, clear letter recognition, and fun interactive learning moments that naturally keep young children engaged from start to finish.
