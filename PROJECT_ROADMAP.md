# Project Roadmap

## Phase 1: Creative Development
- Finalize character sheet for Ziggy
- Confirm color palette and park environment design
- Approve art style direction
- Lock scene sequencing for ABC learning flow

## Phase 2: Storyboard & Animatics
- Create scene-by-scene storyboard
- Define key camera movements
- Block timing for dialogue and songs
- Review educational pacing and interaction moments

## Phase 3: Asset Production
- Model and rig Ziggy
- Create park environment props and backdrops
- Design letters, apples, balls, cats, dogs, elephants, fish, grapes, hats, ice cream, juice
- Build looping animations for friendly motion and expressions

## Phase 4: Audio Production
- Record Ziggy voice-over in warm, clear preschool tone
- Compose upbeat musical bed
- Add sound effects for bouncing, clapping, giggling, and motion cues
- Mix final audio to fit an age-appropriate learning experience

## Phase 5: Final Assembly
- Edit shots in sequence
- Sync dialogue, animation, music, and sound effects
- Review pacing for young children
- Final polish pass and quality control

## Phase 6: Delivery
- Export final master video
- Prepare delivery variants if needed
- Archive source assets and production notes
